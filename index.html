How to use Accounts & Wallets (click to open)

1. Add your accounts

  1. Press + Add account. Create one for each place you keep money: Cash Wallet, your bank, bKash, Nagad, Rocket, Upay.
  2. Type the real balance today in Opening balance and keep Opening balance as of as today. Only records dated on or after that day change the balance.
  3. Use the same name as the payment method you already use (e.g. “Cash”, “bKash”) so old and new income/expense entries are matched automatically.

2. Income and expenses

Income adds to an account and expenses subtract from it. Records tied to an account (bills marked paid) use that account. Older records are matched by payment method name.

3. Move money between your accounts

  1. Press ⇄ Transfer money, choose From and To, enter the amount and date.
  2. One transfer record lowers the first account and raises the second. It is not income or an expense, so your totals stay correct.
  3. Wrong amount? Press Edit. Cancelled? Press Void (you can Restore it). Balances correct themselves, nothing is double counted.

4. Check and manage

  1. History lists every income, expense and transfer for that account.
  2. Deactivate hides an account from transfers and bills but keeps its history and balance.
  3. If a balance differs from your real wallet, check the opening balance and its date, then the history.

Good to know

Balances are calculated, never typed in, which is why edits are safe. Savings goals linked to an account follow its balance. Net Worth adds all account balances, so a savings account is counted once.
